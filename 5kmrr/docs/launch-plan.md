# AgentOps Fieldkit Launch Plan

## Position

AgentOps Fieldkit helps engineering teams make AI coding-agent work accountable in real repos.

The buyer is an engineering leader at a 5-50 engineer startup where developers already use Cursor, Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, local models, or internal agent wrappers. They are feeling coordination waste before they have a formal platform team for it.

## Offer

Primary offer:

> I will spend one week turning your AI coding-agent usage into an inspectable workflow your team can trust: shared state, run receipts, worktree lanes, review gates, cost routing, and handoff rules. If the audit finds no material coordination waste, you do not buy the sprint.

## First 30 Days

### Week 1: Proof and first conversations

- Publish the landing page.
- Pin the new offer from the existing X profile.
- DM or email 30 engineering leaders who publicly talk about coding agents, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, worktrees, or AI-generated code review.
- Offer 5 free 25-minute audits in exchange for permission to anonymize workflow lessons.
- Close one $950 audit or one $4,500 sprint.

### Week 2: Convert one audit into implementation

- Deliver a concrete failure map.
- Show three receipts from their real workflow.
- Offer the 7-day sprint with the audit fee credited.
- Publish one anonymized before/after field note.

### Week 3: Turn delivery into a repeatable kit

- Extract reusable templates:
  - run receipt shape
  - worktree lane policy
  - agent task handoff contract
  - review gate checklist
  - cost routing rules
- Close a retainer for weekly run review and state hygiene.

### Week 4: Repeat into MRR

- Use the first case study to pitch 50 more targeted prospects.
- Ask every audit lead for two referrals.
- Target three retainers at $1,750/month.

## Kill Criteria

Stop or rework the offer if 30 targeted conversations produce:

- fewer than 5 calls,
- no one can name a current agent coordination failure,
- no one will pay for the audit,
- the pain is only curiosity rather than operational waste.

## Keep

Keep the voice concrete:

- real repos
- files touched
- commands run
- failed checks
- cost per verified outcome
- state that survives agent swaps

Avoid:

- generic AI transformation language
- vague productivity claims
- influencer-style founder language
- dashboards before workflow proof
